A Material Design divider component. Renders Google's `<md-gb-divider>` (@material/web); colours, type and shape come from the theme's tokens — never style it with literals.

## Props

Boolean props are real booleans (`disabled`, not `disabled="true"`). Camel-case names (`softDisabled`, `activeTabIndex`) are the element's properties.
- `vertical`: `boolean` — Whether or not the divider is vertical
