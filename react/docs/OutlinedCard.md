A card component. Renders Google's `<md-outlined-card>` (@material/web); colours, type and shape come from the theme's tokens — never style it with literals.

## Composition

Children are your own content; pad it with your own wrapper.

## Slots

- default (children)

## Props

Boolean props are real booleans (`disabled`, not `disabled="true"`). Camel-case names (`softDisabled`, `activeTabIndex`) are the element's properties.
