Material Symbols Rounded matches M3 Expressive's soft geometry. Use the ligature name as text inside .md-icon. Outline by default; the FILL axis switches to 1 for the selected/active state (toggle buttons and nav items do this automatically). Sizes: 20 (xs/s buttons), 24 (default), 32 and 40 (large controls).
