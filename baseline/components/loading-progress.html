Linear progress — wavy and flat
Circular progress — wavy and flat

The loading indicator is for short waits (under ~5s) with no known duration: a shape that rotates while morphing through seven library shapes. Pull-to-refresh and in-button loading use it too. For longer or measurable work use progress; the wavy variant adds energy for playful surfaces, the flat one stays for dense UI. The track ends in a 4dp stop dot; a gap always separates active and inactive.
