Navigation bar — vertical items (compact)
Navigation bar — horizontal items (medium)
Navigation rail — collapsed and expanded

The flexible navigation bar is 64dp (shorter than M3's 80) and puts icon and label side by side on medium windows. The navigation rail collapses to 96dp and expands to a labelled 220dp+ column that replaces the navigation drawer; its active indicator stretches with the item. The indicator pill springs in on selection, and the active icon fills.
