labs/gb · md-gb-*

Expressive actions

The Expressive set lives under @material/web/labs/gb. Buttons, icon buttons and split buttons share one five-step size scale, xs to xl. They are round by default and square on request. Press one and its corners tighten. Selected toggles morph from round to square. Every colour on this page is a Material system role supplied by the theme, so the accent picker restyles all of them.
