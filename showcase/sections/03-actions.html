Buttons, FABs & chips

The stable action set: five common buttons, four icon-button styles, FABs, the four chip types and the labs segmented button. Every one of them is the stock @material/web element with no per-component overrides: emphasis comes from --md-sys-color-primary, secondary-container, tertiary-container and the surface roles the theme supplies, so the accent picker repaints the lot.
