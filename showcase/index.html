Expressive actions

The Expressive set lives under @material/web/labs/gb. Buttons, icon buttons and split buttons share one five-step size scale, xs to xl. They are round by default and square on request. Press one and its corners tighten. Selected toggles morph from round to square. Every colour on this page is a Material system role supplied by the theme, so the accent picker restyles all of them.

Expressive selection & containment

The rest of the labs md-gb-* set: selection controls, cards, lists, menus, badges and dividers. Each one is Google's element as shipped. It reads Material's system roles (--md-sys-color-*, --md-sys-shape-*, --md-sys-typescale-*), and the theme fills those roles in, so changing the accent recolours every control below. The controls are form-associated, so a plain <label> wrapped around one works as its label.

Inputs & selection

Every control below is a stock @material/web form element — text fields, selects, checkboxes, radios, switches and sliders. None of them carries a style of its own here: containers, outlines, focus indicators, error red and the active accent all come from the theme's --md-sys-* roles, so changing the accent above recolours every handle, track and caret at once. They are form-associated, so they submit with a plain <form>.
